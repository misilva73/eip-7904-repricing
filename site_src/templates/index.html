EIP-7904 Repricing Analysis

EIP-7904 reprices a set of EVM opcodes and precompiles. This site reports an empirical re-derivation of those gas costs: NNLS regressions fit on client benchmark runtimes, anchored at 100 Mgas/s, with the worst-case client selected per gas parameter. The methodology page documents the full pipeline.

Of the {{ proposed | length }} gas parameters analysed, {{ n_up }} warrant an increase and {{ n_down }} could be reduced relative to the current cost table. The largest pressure is concentrated in the modular-arithmetic opcodes and several precompiles; see the new-gas proposal for the per-parameter breakdown.
